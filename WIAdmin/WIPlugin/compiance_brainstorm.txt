No — compliance does not only cover catering and hospitality.

“Compliance” is a broad concept used across many industries. It simply means following laws, regulations, standards, and internal policies that apply to a business or activity.

Main Industries Where Compliance Is Important

4

Patient data protection

4

Workplace health & safety

So building a compliance plugin for wicms, would mean covering all different types of businesses

Not necessarily. A compliance plugin for WICMS does not have to cover every industry. In fact, trying to cover all compliance types at once would make the plugin extremely complex and difficult to maintain.

A better approach is to design it as a core compliance framework with industry-specific modules.

1. Core Compliance System (Generic – Works for Any Business)

This is the base plugin that every site can use regardless of industry.

Typical core features:

Policy management

store policies (health & safety, privacy, etc.)

Document management

upload compliance documents

Task / checklist system

compliance checks

Audit logs

record inspections or reviews

Staff acknowledgement

staff confirm they have read policies

Reminders

document renewal dates

Incident reporting

report issues or violations

Role permissions

who can manage compliance

Example core database structure:

WICompliance
│
├── compliance_items
├── compliance_documents
├── compliance_tasks
├── compliance_logs
├── compliance_acknowledgements
└── compliance_incidents


This works for any business.

2. Industry Modules (Optional Add-ons)

Then you add industry-specific compliance modules.

Each module adds industry-specific checklists, rules, and templates.
