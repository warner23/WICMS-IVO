COMPLYWISE HOSPITALITY
Hospitality Compliance Plugin for WICMS

A structured, scalable compliance management system for catering & hospitality businesses


------------------------------------------------------------
1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW
------------------------------------------------------------

ComplyWise Hospitality is a compliance management system designed for the catering and hospitality sector.

It helps businesses to:
- Set up legally
- Operate safely
- Maintain compliance
- Provide evidence when required
- Stay audit/inspection ready

NOTE:
This system does NOT guarantee compliance. It is a management and support system.


------------------------------------------------------------
2. CORE PRODUCT PROMISE
------------------------------------------------------------

“A hospitality compliance operating system that helps businesses set up, run, evidence, review, and maintain legal and best-practice compliance.”
