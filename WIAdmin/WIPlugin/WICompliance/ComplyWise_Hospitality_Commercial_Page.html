The problem

Hospitality businesses often run compliance through paper folders, loose spreadsheets, scattered messages, and memory. That creates missed checks, weak evidence trails, and unnecessary stress before inspections.

  • Missed daily checks and overdue reviews
  • Evidence spread across paper, email, and local devices
  • No clear reminder system for recurring obligations
  • Weak retention and archive handling
  • Limited visibility across multiple locations

The solution

ComplyWise Hospitality turns compliance into a structured operational workflow. It helps teams set up correctly, complete work on time, capture evidence, manage documents, and keep an inspection-ready audit trail.

  • Business-type aware setup wizard
  • Dynamic checklists and recurring logs
  • Documents, policies, and acknowledgements
  • Reminder engine with priorities and escalation
  • Archive and retention controls
