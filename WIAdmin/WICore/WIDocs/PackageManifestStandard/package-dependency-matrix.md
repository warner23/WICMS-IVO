# Package Dependency Matrix - Working Standard

## WI Starter Pack

Required plugins:
- WIProfile
- WIOrg
- WIHR
- WINotifications
- WIScheduler
- WITaskEngine

Optional add-ons:
- WIReportsAnalytics

Sidebar entries:
- Profile
- Organisation
- HR / People
- Tasks
- Schedule
- Notifications

## WIKitchenCompli Basic

Required plugins:
- WICompliance
- WIKitchenCompli
- WIOrg
- WIHR
- WINotifications
- WIScheduler
- WITaskEngine

Core content:
- Food Safety
- Cleaning & Hygiene
- Temperature Control
- Incident Reporting
- Basic Document Control

## WIKitchenCompli Advanced

Includes Basic plus:
- HACCP
- COSHH
- Allergen Control
- Supplier / Delivery Checks
- Equipment Safety
- Training & Certification
- Legal Register
- Corrective Actions
- Audit / Inspection Readiness
- Fire Safety References
- Health & Safety References
- Waste Management
- Maintenance / Service Records
- WIAlerts
- WIReportsAnalytics

## WIKitchenCompli Complete

Includes Advanced plus premium add-ons:
- WIOfflinePro
- WIInspectorPro
- WISMSAlerts
- Automation Engine
- Reports / Export Packs

Sidebar rule:
- Offline Pro appears inside Compliance > Offline / Sync only when enabled.
- Inspector Pro appears inside Compliance > Inspector / Inspection Packs only when enabled.
- SMS Alerts appears inside Notifications > Channels or Compliance > Alert Channels.
- Reports can be a user-facing Reports workspace when enabled.

## Hospitality Operations Pack

Required / first candidates:
- WISpecs
- WIRotas
- WIStock
- WIProfitEngine

Optional expansion:
- WIRestaurant
- WIRooms
- WIBookings
- WIOrderingSuppliers

Rule:
Each must work standalone and expose optional bridges to other installed plugins.
